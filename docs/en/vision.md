# Product vision (EN)

**Product goal:** A Chilean taxpayer, once properly authorized, can issue and trace an electronic tax document through an auditable and secure API, with reliable error handling and an easy-to-use bilingual portal.

## Customer and job
First users: Chilean developers integrating ERPs and small businesses needing programmable invoicing. First value: safe document lifecycle, not another uncontrolled XML generator.

## First vertical slice (MVP)
Synthetic issuer & recipient fixtures; DTE 33/34 model and calculations; CAF/folio simulation before real authorization; XML validation/signing against verified official specifications; SII sandbox connector when credentials and authorization exist; state machine; minimal invoice and operations MFEs; reliable persisted events; local installation.

## Out of scope for initial slice
Payment custody, acquiring, billing subscriptions, cross-border tax support, fully active-active multi-cloud, claims of SII certification, production invoicing, real taxpayer data and always-on AWS.

## Competitive differentiation hypotheses
Developer-first API, clear error messages, auditability, bilingual UX, strong integration contracts, predictable cloud operating cost. Validate with interviews; do not claim product-market fit.

## Business & career outcomes
- Product: one complete, tested Chilean DTE journey with official test evidence where available.
- Career: demonstrate Java/Spring, SQL, REST, distributed events, React/TypeScript, Docker, AWS IAM/S3/Lambda, ALB and Terraform with reproducible proof.
- Business: pilot only after legitimate legal, tax, security and customer prerequisites.

See roadmap and backlog. These are goals, not delivered capabilities.
