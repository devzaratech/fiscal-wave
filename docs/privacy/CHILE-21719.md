## Current effective-date uncertainty / Vigencia y proyecto de prórroga
The [published Law 21.719](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=1209272) currently specifies **1 December 2026** for entry into force. Bill **18.623-07**, requesting postponement, was approved by the Senate in late October 2026 but is **not enacted as of this review**; it remained under congressional consideration. **Do not treat any alternative date (including 2027) as law until publication.** / No asumir postergación vigente. Recheck official BCN/Congress source before release. The prior [Law 19.628](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=141599) remains relevant until the amendments enter into force.

## Role decision / Roles de tratamiento
For each processing operation, separately determine whether:
- FiscalWave operating company is **controller / responsable** (e.g. platform account, marketing, its own analytics and security data).
- FiscalWave is **processor / encargado** for a customer's DTE or tax recipient records under documented instructions, subject to actual contractual facts.
- A third party is processor or separate controller; inventory all hosting, email, AI, support, observability and backup processors.
A vendor role cannot be declared solely by software architecture; contract and factual control over purposes/means determine obligations. / Decisión jurídica según contratos y hechos.

## Fines and real risk / Multas
[Law 21.719 Art. 35](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=1209272) contemplates up to **5,000 / 10,000 / 20,000 UTM** for minor/serious/very serious infringements; recidivism and certain large-company rules can alter exposure. These are statutory ceilings by classification, **not an estimate of what FiscalWave would owe**. / No calcular una multa hipotética como hecho. Technical safeguards, country repos and audits reduce risk but do not immunize against sanctions.

## SII records and privacy rights / Retención tributaria versus supresión
- Legally mandatory, immutable signed DTE XML and evidence cannot be casually erased when tax retention applies; legal counsel must approve the retention basis and precise term. / DTE bajo conservación legal.
- Keep editable user profile/contact information separate from signed XML where feasible; deletion requests should erase only eligible data, mask optional derived data, and document justified exceptions and legal holds. / Separar datos y excepciones.
- Avoid public hashes of RUT/document/subject data: hashes can remain linkable personal data. Never anchor actual DTE or PII on blockchain. / Hash no garantiza anonimato.
- No real PFX, CAF private keys or production DTE in agents/prompts/CI. / Nunca secretos reales en asistentes.

## Dates and deadlines / Plazos
Do not copy GDPR's 72-hour rule as a Chilean general deadline. Requirements for regulator/data-subject notice, response windows, transfer and recordkeeping need source-specific review before automation. / Cada plazo debe verificarse en norma o instrucciones oficiales.

## Preconditions for pilot / Requisitos para piloto
Legal counsel review of role map and policy, signed customer/processor terms, genuine signer and SII authorization, independently verified data-subject rights procedures, per-tenant security tests, incident rehearsal, backup/restore, operational owners and evidence. A country-specific Git repo is not proof of legal compliance. / Sin aval legal y pruebas, no afirmar listo para producción.
